\section{Cosmic Inflation}

Cosmic inflation is a period of exponential expansion in the very early universe, driven by the potential energy of a scalar field $\phi$ called the inflaton.

\subsection{Inflaton Field Dynamics}

The evolution of the inflaton field on a lattice is governed by the Klein--Gordon equation in an expanding background:

\begin{equation}
\ddot{\phi} + 3H\dot{\phi} - \frac{\nabla^2 \phi}{a^2} + V'(\phi) = 0
\end{equation}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\phi$ -- inflaton field value
\item $H = \dot{a}/a$ -- Hubble parameter
\item $a$ -- scale factor
\item $V(\phi)$ -- inflaton potential
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Potential Models}

Three inflaton potentials are implemented:

\begin{equation}
V_{\text{quadratic}}(\phi) = \frac{1}{2} m^2 \phi^2
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
V_{\text{Starobinsky}}(\phi) = \Lambda^4 \left(1 - e^{-\sqrt{2/3}\,\phi}\right)^2
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
V_{\text{hilltop}}(\phi) = V_0 \left(1 - \frac{\phi^2}{\mu^2}\right)^2
\end{equation}

\subsection{Power Spectrum}

The primordial power spectrum $P(k)$ is computed via 3D FFT of the inflaton field perturbations $\delta\phi(\mathbf{x})$:

\begin{equation}
P(k) = \frac{k^3}{2\pi^2} \langle |\delta\hat{\phi}(k)|^2 \rangle
\end{equation}

The spectral index $n_s$ describes the scale dependence:

\begin{equation}
n_s - 1 = \frac{d \ln P(k)}{d \ln k}
\end{equation}
